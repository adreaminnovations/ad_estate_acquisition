Make Confident Land Deals Without Guesswork

Buying land or taking on society redevelopment projects involves complex math—calculating permissible floor area, tenant relocation costs, monthly transit rent, and municipal approval fees.

This module plugs directly into Odoo CRM to help you manage land leads, public tenders, and broker referrals in one clean workspace.

It handles all the math automatically, showing you estimated buildable space, total project costs, projected sales revenue, and expected ROI percentage instantly.

How It Makes Everyday Work Easier

  • Tender & Deal Tracking: Manage public tender deadlines, earnest money deposits (EMD), and broker agreements inside CRM opportunities.
  • Redevelopment Planning: Calculate tenant rehab areas, bonus incentives, and monthly transit rent commitments automatically.
  • Profitability Calculator: Get instant calculations of total construction costs, saleable area, and overall project profit margins.
  • Permit Tracker: Monitor municipal approval stages (IOD, CC, OC) and store signed permit documents safely.

Step-by-Step Usage Guide

Step 1: Log Land Leads & Tenders

Go to CRM > Pipeline or Land Acquisition > Opportunities.

  • Set the deal type to Public Tender, Broker Referral, or Direct Purchase.
  • For tenders, enter submission deadlines, portal links, and deposit (EMD) details.
  • Assign broker contacts to keep track of referral fees.

Step 2: Run a Feasibility Study

Go to Land Acquisition > Feasibility Studies and click New.

  • Input plot size, allowable building area (FSI), and extra TDR area to see total buildable space.
  • For redevelopment, enter existing tenant count, carpet areas, and monthly transit rent.
  • Review calculated figures for total estimated costs, projected sales income, and net ROI %.

Step 3: Track Municipal Sanctions

Go to Land Acquisition > Municipal Sanctions to log approval progress.

  • Track key permits: IOD (Intimation of Disapproval), CC (Commencement Certificate), and OC (Occupancy Certificate).
  • Record approval fees, submission dates, and issue dates.
  • Attach approved architectural drawings and official NOC files directly to the record.

Step 4: Generate Legal Agreements

Use the Generate Document button inside any Feasibility or Sanction record.

  • Select the document type: Letter of Intent (LOI) or Development Agreement (DA).
  • Pick standard legal clauses to auto-populate plot details, owner names, and payment terms.
  • Print or save clean PDF copies ready for signing.
